# Family Meal Options

*Vegetarian-friendly places within ~15 min of Halswell, Christchurch*
*Considerations: Andrew & Dylan vegetarian, Andrew diabetic (type 2)*

## Best Cuisines for Vegetarian + Low Carb

1. **Indian** — Paneer, tofu, dal, spinach/eggplant dishes are protein-heavy. Skip rice/naan.
2. **Middle Eastern** — Falafel, hummus, tabbouleh, grilled halloumi, shakshuka.
3. **Thai** — Tofu curries, stir-fries. Coconut milk is fatty but pastes are low carb. Skip rice.

## Specific Places

### 🇮🇳 Coriander's (Bush Inn)
- 364 Riccarton Rd, Upper Riccarton
- 4.2★ · ~25 min delivery · 100% vegetarian-friendly
- Vegan butter "chicken" $30, Paneer butter masala $26, Paneer tikka $22, Cheese pakora $18
- Vegetarian sizzling platter $32 (for 2)
- Best all-round pick for Andrew

### 🇮🇳 Nana's Indian Cuisine (Hornby)
- 403 Main South Rd, Hornby
- 4.2★ · Cheaper (dinner from $11)
- More meat-focused, limited vegetarian options

### 🇮🇳 Orange House (Upper Riccarton)
- 55 Auburn Ave, Upper Riccarton
- 4.6★ · Chinese/Asian
- Some veggie options (combination vegetables, tofu rice) but mostly carb-heavy
- Good for the family, less ideal for low carb

### 🍜 SUMOS (Riccarton)
- 182 Clarence St, Riccarton
- 4.2★ · Japanese
- Spicy miso vegetarian ramen $22.50, Vegetarian katsu bento $33.50, Agedashi tofu curry $21.50
- Kids would love gyoza, donburi, mochi ice cream
- Noodle/rice heavy but good veggie options

### 🍺 The Halswell
- 344 Halswell Rd, Halswell
- Pub · Kids playground · Family-friendly
- Mediterranean mezze bowl $26 (falafel, hummus, tabbouleh)
- Veggie deluxe burger $26, The Fun Guy pizza $27
- Kids menu $15 (cheeseburger, fish fingers, pasta, pizza)
- 15% public holiday surcharge

### 🍟 Captain Bens (Upper Riccarton)
- 4.5★ · Fish & chips
- Good for Luca who eats meat

### 🍗 Addington Fried Chicken (Addington)
- 4.5★ · Fried chicken
- Good for the meat-eaters in the family
