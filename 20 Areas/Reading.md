### 📰 Articles & Essays

#### Wisdom of Crowds
- [x] ~~**BBC: Wisdom of Crowds**~~
  *When crowd wisdom works and when it fails. Galton's 1907 ox-weight experiment showed group averages can be scarily accurate, but social influence and groupthink can bias crowds badly. Independence of judgment is key.*
  https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20140708-when-crowd-wisdom-goes-wrong

- [x] ~~**MIT Tech Review: Wisdom of the Confident**~~
  *Neurobiologists found that identifying "confident" individuals (less influenced by others' opinions) and using their estimates alone produces better results than full crowd averages. Ignore the followers, listen to the independent thinkers.*
  https://www.technologyreview.com/2014/07/14/172082/forget-the-wisdom-of-crowds-neurobiologists-reveal-the-wisdom-of-the-confident/

### 🌍 History & Politics

- [x] ~~**What Can We Learn From the Montreal Protocol** (Earth Day)~~
  *How the world successfully phased out ozone-depleting CFCs in the 1980s. Shows international cooperation can work on environmental crises, though climate change is a harder problem — it touches every part of the economy, not just a few chemicals.*
  https://www.earthday.org/what-can-we-learn-from-the-montreal-protocol/

- [x] ~~**Texas is the Future of America** (NYTimes)~~
  *Op-ed arguing Texas, not California, is the real bellwether for America's future. Fast-growing, already majority-minority, rapidly urbanizing — its political battles foreshadow the nation's.*
  https://www.nytimes.com/2021/10/05/opinion/texas-census-united-states.html

- [x] ~~**Chomsky Interview** (Jacobin)~~
  *Wide-ranging 2019 interview covering climate change, US imperialism, Venezuela, Iran. Argues the Green New Deal is sound economics but faces enormous political obstacles. Characteristically doom-laden but urgent.*
  https://www.jacobinmag.com/2019/07/noam-chomsky-interview-climate-change-imperialism

- [x] ~~**Rise of the Useless Class** (TED / Yuval Noah Harari)~~
  *AI will create a new "useless class" of people whose labour isn't needed. Challenges the assumption that new jobs always replace old ones. Organisms are just algorithms, silicon or carbon — nothing guarantees humans stay valuable.*
  https://ideas.ted.com/the-rise-of-the-useless-class/

- [x] ~~**The 14 Rules for Eternal Fascism** (Overland)~~
  *Analyses Jordan Peterson through the lens of Eco's 14 features of fascism. Connects Peterson's Hero's Journey rhetoric to fascist aesthetics.*
  https://overland.org.au/2019/02/the-14-rules-for-eternal-fascism-jordan-peterson-and-the-far-right/

- [x] ~~**The Intellectual We Deserve** (Current Affairs)~~
  *Extended 12,000-word takedown of Jordan Peterson as a thinker. Argues he combines obvious truisms with deliberate obscurantism to appear profound.*
  https://www.currentaffairs.org/2018/03/the-intellectual-we-deserve

- [x] ~~**Trade War Article** (Forbes, 2018)~~
  *How the US-China trade war was rearranging supply chains and hurting China's economy. Now somewhat dated by subsequent events.*
  https://www.forbes.com/sites/kenrapoza/2018/09/19/this-trade-war-is-a-bummer-for-china/

- [x] ~~**HBR: The New New Product Development Game** (1986)~~
  *The classic paper that inspired Scrum and Agile. Takeuchi & Nonaka argue against the old sequential "relay race" model — use cross-functional teams that pass the ball like rugby. Overlapping phases, self-organizing, multilearning. The founding document of modern agile.*
  https://hbr.org/1986/01/the-new-new-product-development-game

- [x] ~~**How McKinsey Destroyed the Middle Class** (The Atlantic)~~
  *Management consulting hollowed out middle management, eliminated organization men, and drove extreme inequality. Consultants taught companies to treat workers as costs to minimize rather than stakeholders to develop.*
  https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2020/02/how-mckinsey-destroyed-middle-class/605878/

- [x] ~~**Friendster Autopsy** (Wired)~~
  *Why Friendster died — it wasn't user count, it was weak connections between users (low "K-core density"). When the 2009 redesign hit, loosely-connected outer users left first, cascading inward. Lesson: networks need strong ties to survive.*
  https://www.wired.com/2013/02/friendster-autopsy/

- [x] ~~**Coronavirus vs Spanish Flu** (NYTimes)~~
  *Spanish flu killed mostly 20-40 year olds via cytokine storm; COVID disproportionately kills elderly. Medicine, hygiene, and surveillance are vastly better now.*
  https://www.nytimes.com/2020/03/09/health/coronavirus-is-very-different-from-the-spanish-flu-of-1918-heres-how.html

- [x] ~~**Sun Yat Sen's Vision for China's Development**~~
  *Archival document.*
  https://archive.org/details/developmentchina00suny

- [x] ~~**About Curb Cuts and Accessibility** (Boom California)~~
  *The history of kerb cuts and how disability rights improvements spill over into mainstream life.*
  https://boomcalifornia.com/2012/06/26/the-peoples-sidewalks/

- [x] ~~**Peeling Back History**~~
  *NZ history blog about early Canterbury settlement: stories of pioneers, the Canterbury Association's 1850 immigration plans. Local Christchurch/Canterbury history focus.*
  http://www.peelingbackhistory.co.nz/

### 💻 Programming & Tech

- [x] ~~**Level Up Your Regex Game** (Medium)~~
  *Practical JavaScript regex tutorial covering character classes, quantifiers, groups, anchors, flags, lookahead/lookbehind. Good intermediate reference.*
  https://medium.com/smelly-code/level-up-your-regex-game-39b0b56fef2a

- [x] ~~**Web Design in 4 Minutes**~~
  *Brilliant, concise CSS tutorial. Covers the essentials: centering, font families, spacing, colour/contrast, balance, gradients, shadows. The CSS framework Bulma was born from this.*
  https://jgthms.com/web-design-in-4-minutes/

- [x] ~~**What Is Code?** (Bloomberg / Paul Ford)~~
  *Legendary 38,000-word explainer of what code is, written for non-programmers. Told as a narrative about a manager trying to understand software. Covers history, culture, economics of software development. A masterpiece of explanatory writing.*
  https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2015-paul-ford-what-is-code/

- [x] ~~**Functors, Applicatives and Monads in Pictures**~~
  *Visual guide to functional programming concepts using Elm diagrams. Good if you're learning FP.*
  https://medium.com/@l.mugnaini/functors-applicatives-and-monads-in-pictures-784c2b5786f7

- [x] ~~**How to Think About Monads** (Gist)~~
  *Text-based explanation of monads. Starts from pure function composition, shows how side effects break it, then shows how monads restore composability. Clearer than the pictures version for some people.*
  https://gist.github.com/cscalfani/b63552922a8deb2656ecd5ec8a1a77a8

- [x] ~~**Scuttlebutt**~~
  *Decentralised social network built by NZ developer Dominic Tarr. Works offline, syncs peer-to-peer when you meet people. Anti-corporate, pro-privacy, local-first. Small network but interesting tech.*
  https://scuttlebutt.nz/

- [x] ~~**Meet the Counterantidisintermediationists** (The Atlantic)~~
  *Profile of Dominic Tarr and the Scuttlebutt ecosystem — the people building decentralised alternatives to platform capitalism.*
  https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2017/05/meet-the-counterantidisintermediationists/527553/

- [x] ~~**HTTP Overview** (MDN)~~
  *Standard Mozilla reference doc. Covers HTTP as client-server protocol, components (proxies, caches), HTTP/2, evolution from HTTP/0.9 to HTTP/3. Good reference material.*
  https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Overview

- [x] ~~**Map Reduce** (IBM)~~
  *Overview of MapReduce distributed computing paradigm.*
  https://www.01.ibm.com/software/data/infosphere/hadoop/mapreduce/

### 🌱 Regenerative Agriculture

- [x] ~~**Rodale Institute: Why Regenerative Agriculture**~~
  *"Regenerative" goes beyond "sustainable" — it actively improves soil health rather than just maintaining it. Prioritises soil health, animal welfare, and worker fairness. Led to the Regenerative Organic Certified standard.*
  https://rodaleinstitute.org/why-organic/organic-basics/regenerative-organic-agriculture/

- [x] ~~**Redirecting Agricultural Subsidies** (WRI)~~
  *Governments spend $600B/year on agricultural subsidies, but only 5% supports conservation. Redirecting even a portion could massively improve food security and reduce emissions.*
  https://www.wri.org/insights/redirecting-agricultural-subsidies-sustainable-food-future

### 🧠 ADHD & Mind

- [x] ~~**Brain Training & Neurofeedback for ADHD** (ADDitude)~~
  *FAQ on whether brain training apps and neurofeedback work for ADHD. Short answer: mixed evidence. Working memory training has some support. Lumosity-type apps are mostly marketing. Neurofeedback shows promise but is expensive and inconsistent.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/adhd-brain-training-neurofeedback-memory/

- [x] ~~**CBT for ADHD** (ADDitude)~~
  *Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for ADHD adults. Addresses "cognitive distortions" — negative self-talk patterns built up from years of setbacks. CBT restructures automatic negative thoughts like "I never do anything right." Good evidence base and directly relevant.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-adhd/

- [x] ~~**Decision Making with ADHD** (ADDitude)~~
  *ADHDers are great at "hot decisions" (crisis situations — dopamine fires up) but terrible at "cold decisions" (deliberate, information-heavy choices). Explains executive function scaffolding and how to compensate with structure.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/decision-making-with-adhd/

- [x] ~~**Procrastination Stories** (ADDitude)~~
  *Personal essay about ADHD-related procrastination. Found a folder of letters never replied to, including one to his deceased grandmother. "20 years of deep guilt." Practical advice: draw a line, start leaving notes, follow through.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/procrastination-stories/

- [x] ~~**Anxiety in Adults with ADHD** (ADDitude / Dr. Ned Hallowell)~~
  *25-40% of adults with ADHD also have an anxiety disorder. ADHD creates plenty to worry about. Anxiety can actually feel stimulating for ADHD brains seeking mental focus. Treat ADHD first, then the anxiety patterns.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/signs-of-anxiety-in-adults-with-adhd/

- [x] ~~**Analysis Paralysis — 13 Ways to Beat ADHD Paralysis** (ADDitude)~~
  *Tips for beating decision paralysis: limit options to 3, use "good enough" threshold, set time limits, flip a coin (you'll know your preference by how you feel about the result).*
  https://www.additudemag.com/slideshows/analysis-paralysis-and-adhd-trouble-making-decisions/

- [x] ~~**Sleep Help for ADHD Adults** (ADDitude)~~
  *Reasons ADHDers can't sleep: racing brain, medication timing, circadian rhythm disruption. Tips: try stimulant before bed (paradoxically helps some people), consistent schedule, track sleep patterns.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/slideshows/i-cant-sleep-help-for-adhd-adults/

- [x] ~~**Productivity Tips for ADHD Brains** (ADDitude)~~
  *Practical tips: everything takes 2x longer than you think, forget perfect, prep your environment, visible deadlines, body doubling, work in bursts with immediate rewards.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/slideshows/productivity-tips/

- [x] ~~**How to Delegate Your To-Do List** (ADDitude)~~
  *Why ADHDers struggle to delegate: can't give clear instructions, don't feel entitled to ask. Solution: map out the task steps first (draw a diagram), then ask the person for feedback on your instructions before handing it off.*
  https://www.additudemag.com/to-do-list-delegate-adult-adhd/

### 🤝 Society & Culture

- [x] ~~**Chinese Philosophy & Mental Health** (Psyche)~~
  *Ancient Chinese philosophy recognised mental illness as communal, not just individual. The Huangdi Neijing (4th century BCE) described disorders caused by community dysfunction. Modern Western psychiatry over-emphasises the individual.*
  https://psyche.co/ideas/chinese-philosophy-has-long-known-that-mental-health-is-communal

- [x] ~~**Essay Exploring Why Intentional Communities Fail** (Aeon)~~
  *Long essay on why communes and eco-villages fail at about the same rate as start-ups. Core problem: they reproduce the same interpersonal dynamics (ego, power, jealousy) they're trying to escape.*
  https://aeon.co/essays/like-start-ups-most-intentional-communities-fail-why

- [x] ~~**Rochdale Principles** (Wikipedia)~~
  *The 7 cooperative principles (voluntary membership, democratic control, member economic participation, autonomy, education, cooperation between co-ops, concern for community). Originated in 1844 Rochdale, UK.*
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_Principles

- [x] ~~**100 Māori Words** (NZ History)~~
  *Reference list of essential te reo Māori vocabulary with audio pronunciation: marae terms, greetings, body parts, concepts. Practical NZ resource.*
  https://nzhistory.govt.nz/culture/maori-language-week/100-maori-words

- [x] ~~**Bob Jones is Not Just a Racist, He's Also a Coward** (The Spinoff)~~
  *NZ High Court defamation case where Bob Jones sued a Māori woman for calling his column racist. He withdrew during cross-examination. Vivid courtroom reporting of Jones making a fool of himself.*
  https://thespinoff.co.nz/atea/14-02-2020/bob-jones-is-not-just-a-racist-hes-also-a-coward/

- [x] ~~**Living the Moral High Life** (The Spinoff)~~
  *A week of consuming only from social enterprises in Auckland. Ate at Everybody Eats (pay-what-you-can), used Eat My Lunch (buy one, donate one). Found social enterprises hard to identify and the ecosystem fragmented.*
  https://thespinoff.co.nz/business/30-09-2017/living-the-moral-high-life-a-week-consuming-consciously/
